Good food makes a surf trip better.

Honest local picks for breakfast, post-surf food, dog-friendly pubs, family meals and coastal drinks near the places you actually want to surf.

Places worth stopping for.

Surfbooker reviews food and drink spots that genuinely improve a beach day, surf trip or coastal break. Our full venue reviews currently focus on Devon and North Somerset, while our UK beach guides provide practical eating and drinking pointers for surf destinations across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

We focus on the details that matter when you are actually visiting: location, parking, dogs, families, atmosphere, value and whether the food is worth making time for.
