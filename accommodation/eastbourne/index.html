About this destination

Planning a stay in Eastbourne.

Eastbourne's seafront curves north-east from the foot of the South Downs, with Beachy Head's chalk cliffs rising just west of town. The beach is shingle, with sand showing at low tide, and the Victorian pier sits roughly in the middle. Designed by Eugenius Birch and opened in 1870, the pier lost much of its central building to a fire in 2014, though around two-thirds of the structure was saved.
