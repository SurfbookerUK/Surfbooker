About this destination

Planning a stay in Newquay.

Newquay gives visitors a larger choice of accommodation, food, lessons and hire than a small surf village. It is useful when a group wants several beaches or evening options, but the town is spread out enough that property location matters.

Best for

Surf trips, groups, nightlife and trips without relying entirely on one beach

Setting

Lively coastal town with year-round services

Getting to the surf

Several beaches sit around town; the practical route depends on where you stay
