About this destination

Planning a stay in Southbourne.

Southbourne is the eastern end of Bournemouth's long beach, below the cliff-top suburb and its high street. You reach it straight from the shops through Fisherman's Walk, or down the cliff paths and zig-zags. It's a Blue Flag beach with wide areas of sand and shingle, and because it's well away from the piers and the town centre it is noticeably quieter on busy summer days.
