About this destination

Planning a stay in White Park Bay.

White Park Bay is a white arc of sand between two chalk headlands on the North Antrim coast, backed by ancient dunes and cared for by the National Trust since 1938. It lies off the B146, 6.5 miles from Bushmills and 7.5 miles from Ballycastle with the hamlet of Portbraddan at its western end. The National Trust car park is open all day, and the bay itself is open from dawn to dusk.
