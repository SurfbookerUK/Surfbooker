About this destination

Planning a stay in Hayle.

Hayle is useful for visitors who want town services and access to several parts of St Ives Bay. It should be treated as a broad base rather than a single beach resort because accommodation and beach entrances are spread across the area.

Best for

Families, longer stays and exploring several beaches

Setting

Working town beside an estuary and dune-backed coast

Getting to the surf

The bay is extensive; a car helps visitors choose between access points
