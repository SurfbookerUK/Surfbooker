About this destination

Planning a stay in Northam.

Northam suits visitors who want a calmer residential base, open space and easy access to Northam Burrows. Westward Ho! beach, surf lessons and hire remain close, while the village offers a quieter place to return to after a day outside.

Best for

Quieter stays, dog walks, families and outdoor time

Setting

Residential, open and close to the Burrows

Getting to the surf

Short walk or drive to Westward Ho! depending on the property
