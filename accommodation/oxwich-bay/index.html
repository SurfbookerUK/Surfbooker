About this destination

Planning a stay in Oxwich Bay.

Oxwich Bay is a 2.5-mile (4km) curve of sand on the south of the Gower, reached from the small village of Oxwich. Behind the dunes is a wetland that forms Oxwich Burrows National Nature Reserve, home to rare plants, insects and birds, so the bay feels wilder than its car park suggests. In 2007 it was even named the most beautiful beach in Britain.
