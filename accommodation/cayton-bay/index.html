About this destination

Planning a stay in Cayton Bay.

Cayton Bay is a cliff-backed bay roughly four miles south of Scarborough, one of the string of sandy bays along this stretch of coast between Scarborough and Filey. The council describes it as a small sandy bay backed by cliffs, with a mix of sand and rock, and it's a long-standing favourite with surfers and with visitors from the holiday park above.
