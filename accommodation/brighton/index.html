About this destination

Planning a stay in Brighton.

Brighton's seafront is a long run of steep shingle broken up by groynes, with Brighton Palace Pier in the middle and the rusting frame of the West Pier off the Hove end. The West Pier opened in 1866, closed to the public in 1975, and was wrecked by storms and fires in the early 2000s. Today only a metal skeleton is left, with the i360 tower on the promenade beside it.
