About this destination

Planning a stay in Harlyn Bay.

Harlyn Bay is a sandy, north-facing beach about three miles west of Padstow, between Trevone to the east and Cataclews Point to the west. It is backed by low dunes and cliffs, with a shop, café and pub right by the sand. The village behind is small and quiet for much of the year, and the area sits within the Cornwall Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty.
