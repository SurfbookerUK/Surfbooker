About this destination

Planning a stay in St Ives.

St Ives combines a recognised surf beach at Porthmeor with a much broader town break. It works for mixed-interest groups, although parking and the exact position of accommodation can strongly affect how easy it is to reach the beach.

Best for

Couples, families, food, culture and surf trips

Setting

Popular harbour and arts town
