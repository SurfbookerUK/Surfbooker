About this destination

Planning a stay in Holywell Bay.

Holywell Bay lies about three miles west-south-west of Newquay in the parish of Cubert, and the National Trust calls it the largest bay on this stretch of coast. You reach it on foot from the end of the village through a tall dune system that is around 5,000 years old and one of the largest in Britain. The path is uneven and the sand is soft, so pack with the carry in mind.
