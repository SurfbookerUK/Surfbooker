About this destination

Planning a stay in Sandsend.

Sandsend is the small village at the north-western end of the long sands from Whitby, sitting at the foot of Lythe Bank. Two streams, Sandsend Beck and East Row Beck, run down through Mulgrave Woods and out across the sand. The two hamlets of Sandsend and East Row grew together when cottages were built for workers in the local alum industry.
