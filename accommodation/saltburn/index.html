About this destination

Planning a stay in Saltburn.

Saltburn-by-the-Sea is a Victorian resort on the Teesside coast. Henry Pease imagined a town on the clifftop in 1858, and his family's company built it. Below the town, the pier opened in 1869 and is the only surviving pleasure pier on the north-east and Yorkshire coast, while a water-balanced cliff tramway still carries you between the promenade and the town.
