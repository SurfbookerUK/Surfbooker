About this destination

Planning a stay in Newgale.

Newgale works particularly well as a surf base because staying nearby makes early beach checks and changing conditions easier to manage. The choices below are researched accommodation guides rather than personal Surfbooker reviews.

Best for

Surf trips, families, couples and coast-path breaks

Setting

Long beach, open coast and small settlements

Getting to the surf

Options range from about 100 metres from the beach to roughly one mile inland
