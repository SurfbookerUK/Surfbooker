About this destination

Planning a stay in Redcar.

Redcar's beach runs along the town seafront from Coatham towards Marske, and the RNLI describes it as a busy, gently sloping sandy resort beach close to shops, restaurants and pubs. It's popular for kitesurfing and windsurfing as well as surfing, so the water is often shared.
