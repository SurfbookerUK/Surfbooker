About this destination

Planning a stay in Appledore.

Appledore is a good match for visitors who want character, independent places to eat and a slower village atmosphere between surf sessions. Westward Ho! is an easy drive away, while Appledore itself is best enjoyed on foot.

Best for

Couples, food lovers, character stays and slower coastal weekends

Setting

Historic, independent and village-focused
