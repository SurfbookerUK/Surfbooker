About this destination

Planning a stay in Freshwater Bay.

Freshwater Bay is a small cove at the western end of the Isle of Wight's south coast, with steep chalk cliffs on either side and a promenade along the back. The beach is mostly pebbles, with lumps of chalk washed down from the cliffs. Erosion has shaped the bay's rocks over the years: Arch Rock collapsed in 1992, Mermaid Rock broke away in 1968, and Stag Rock still stands just offshore, named after a stag said to have leapt from the cliff.
