About this destination

Planning a stay in Bude.

Bude combines town facilities with access to several north Cornwall beaches. It can work well for visitors who want food, shops and services close to their accommodation while retaining the option to choose between Summerleaze and Widemouth Bay.

Best for

Longer stays and visitors comparing north Cornwall beaches

Setting

Coastal town with everyday services

Getting to the surf

Summerleaze is in town; other beaches are easier by road
