About this destination

Planning a stay in Towan Beach.

Towan is the flat, sandy beach right in the middle of Newquay, at the bottom of a steep hill below the town centre. Newquay Harbour sits on its western side and gives it shelter, which is why Visit Cornwall calls it the town's most sheltered beach. At low tide the sand runs on to Great Western and Tolcarne, and the coves join up into one long stretch you can walk along.
