About this destination

Planning a stay in Langland Bay.

Langland Bay sits just west of Mumbles, the first of the sandy bays as you head out along the south Gower from Swansea. A promenade lined with traditional beach huts runs along the back, with cliffs and a golf course behind, and the bay is a designated conservation area. It is a short drive from Swansea along winding roads, with two large car parks behind the beach.
