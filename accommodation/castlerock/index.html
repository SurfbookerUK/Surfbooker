About this destination

Planning a stay in Castlerock.

Castlerock's beach runs for about a kilometre between the sea cliffs of Downhill to the west and the Barmouth, where the River Bann meets the sea, to the east. The village sits right behind it, and Castlerock Golf Club fills the dunes east of the entrance. The beach regularly holds a Blue Flag. On clear days you can look west to the Inishowen peninsula in Donegal.
