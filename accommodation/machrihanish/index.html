About this destination

Planning a stay in Machrihanish.

Machrihanish Bay is a long Atlantic-facing beach on the west coast of Kintyre, running about three miles north from Machrihanish village to Westport. From the south end you look out to Gigha, Islay and Jura, and the village is just north of the Mull of Kintyre.
