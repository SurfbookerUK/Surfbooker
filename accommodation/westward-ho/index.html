About this destination

Planning a stay in Westward Ho!.

Westward Ho! is a straightforward choice for surf weekends, family breaks and first-time surf trips. The beach, surf lessons, equipment hire, cafés, places to eat and evening drinks are close together, so it is easy to spend less time driving and more time by the water.

Best for

Surf weekends, families and first-time surf trips

Setting

Beachfront, practical and easy-going

Getting to the surf

Much of the village is walkable to Westward Ho! beach
