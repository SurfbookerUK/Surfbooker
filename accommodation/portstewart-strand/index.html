About this destination

Planning a stay in Portstewart Strand.

Portstewart Strand is two miles of National Trust sand, known locally simply as The Strand, running from the edge of Portstewart towards the mouth of the River Bann. It is backed by dunes around 6,000 years old, protected as an Area of Special Scientific Interest, and from the beach you can see Mussenden Temple on the cliffs across the river.
