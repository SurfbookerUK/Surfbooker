At a glance

Practical property details.

Type of stay
Caravan and camping park with holiday homes and wigwams
Location
Belhaven Bay, Coldingham Bay and Pease Bay, Scotland
Accommodation
Touring and tent pitches, caravan holiday homes (including an easy access unit), wigwam glamping pods and Benston Cottage, a seaside cottage on Edinburgh Road
Suitable for
Campers, motorhome and caravan owners, families and groups wanting simple glamping
Confirmed features
Shower blocks, kitchen area, children's play park, reception, fire pits at the wigwams, easy access holiday home with ramped entrance and wet-room bathroom
Parking
Parking on Shore Road, opposite the seaside cottage, for beach access
Beach access
The seaside cottage is described as a short stroll from the beach
