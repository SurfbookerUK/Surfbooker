Beach shuttle

Newgale Holidays advertises a shuttle service to Newgale Beach, Nolton Haven and the Pembrokeshire Coast Path. Confirm times, capacity and luggage arrangements directly; carriage of surfboards is not currently verified.

Accessibility

Three of the four cottages are described by the operator as single-level and wheelchair accessible. Ramsey is specifically confirmed as wheelchair accessible; confirm the most suitable cottage directly before booking.

Why it works for a Newgale surf trip

The site trades immediate beach access for countryside space, parking and a dog exercise area while keeping Newgale roughly one mile away.
