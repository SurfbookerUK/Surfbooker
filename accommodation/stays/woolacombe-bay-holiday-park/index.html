Woolacombe Bay Holiday Park

At a glance

Practical property details.

Type of stay
Holiday park with caravans, lodges and apartments
Location
Woolacombe and Putsborough, North Devon
Accommodation
291 units: graded caravans, Surf Village lodges with decking and private hot tubs, sea-view apartments and a farmhouse. No camping or touring. Open 20 March to 2 November 2026.
Suitable for
Families, groups, dog owners (some caravans), guests wanting on-site leisure and entertainment.
Confirmed features
indoor and outdoor heated pools, waterslides, water sports lake, spa with thermal suite, gym, cinema, live entertainment, amusements, bars and restaurants, convenience store, EV charging
Parking
Parking beside lodges; Surf Village units have private parking for two cars. EV charging in main car park plus lodge chargers.
Beach access
About 15 minutes' steep walk to Woolacombe beach (stiles, not pushchair friendly), or about 5 minutes by car.
Dogs
Some caravans dog-friendly; dogs on leads in most outdoor areas, not in pools, spa or entertainment venues.
