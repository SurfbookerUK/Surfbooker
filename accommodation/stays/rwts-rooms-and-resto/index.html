Introduction

A seafront base for Broad Haven beach days.

Rwts combines guest rooms with a restaurant in a seafront position at Broad Haven. The beach is close by, so the location could work well for travellers who want to check the conditions, get to the sand without a long drive and have food on site after a session. Sea-view rooms are available and some rooms have balconies, but these features do not apply to every room; confirm the room details when booking.

Room options

About the rooms.

Rwts describes nine coastal-inspired guest rooms. Every room has its own en-suite shower room. Sea-view rooms can be booked and some rooms include balconies, so it is worth checking the exact room rather than assuming either feature is included.

Room 4 is the family room, sleeping four in one double bed and a bunk bed. Selected ground-floor rooms welcome dogs, so include this requirement in your enquiry.

Food and drink

An on-site, largely plant-focused restaurant.

Rwts’ restaurant is largely plant-focused, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices. The current offering includes pizzas and ramen bowls. Menus and prices can change, so ask for the current menu when planning your visit. Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the restaurant when kept on leads and comfortable around other guests.

Surfing from here

Plan Broad Haven beach days from this listing.

Broad Haven is an open, west-facing sandy bay used by surfers when conditions are right. Its exposure means wave size and quality change with swell, wind and tide, and a visit is not guaranteed to produce beginner-friendly surf. Check the forecast, local safety information and conditions on the day; book a lesson when you would benefit from equipment, coaching and a local decision about the best venue.

Surf Master Class and Shaka Surf Women both operate in the wider area, while Haven Sports is based in Broad Haven. Meeting beaches and equipment availability can change with the conditions, so confirm arrangements before travelling.

Explore Broad Haven

Useful additions to a surf-trip plan.

Little Haven

Walk south towards Little Haven by the coast. The beach route depends on the tide, so check tide times and use the coast path when the sand route is cut off.

Newgale and the Coast Path

Newgale is a more exposed beach to assess against the forecast. Broad Haven also sits directly on the Pembrokeshire Coast Path.

Days away from the beach

Options include seasonal Skomer trips, Sweet Home Alpaca, Picton Castle Gardens, St Davids and advance-booked pottery painting in Haverfordwest.

Broad Haven’s northern third has seasonal dog restrictions from 1 May to 30 September. Check current signs when you arrive.
