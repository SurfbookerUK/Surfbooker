Intro

Wake up, check the surf, walk onto the beach.

Rwts sits on the Broad Haven seafront, just metres from the water, with its own car park behind the building. Sea-view rooms look straight out over St Brides Bay, Some have a balcony for watching the sun go down.

Downstairs, the restaurant serves breakfast, lunch and dinner, so a hot coffee or a meal is never far after a session. Rwts also runs a live surf cam, so you can check the waves before you head out.

Room guide

Choose the room that fits the trip.

Rooms 1-3 are ground-floor rooms that welcome dogs.

Room 4 is the family room, sleeps four with a double bed and a bunk bed.

Rooms 5 - 9 are the coastal rooms.

Surfing from here

A west-facing bay on your doorstep.

Broad Haven is a long sandy bay facing west into St Brides Bay. It is a proper all-rounder: gentle summer waves for first-timers, and bigger, punchier surf when the swell builds through autumn and winter. Exposure means wave size and quality change with swell, wind and tide, so check the forecast and conditions on the day.

Summer

Small, forgiving waves that suit beginners, with lifeguards patrolling from late June to early September.

Autumn and winter

More swell and more power. Southerly weather rolling in past the headland lights the bay up for experienced surfers.

Low tide

A big stretch of firm sand opens up, and you can walk round the headland to Little Haven, tide permitting.

For lessons and hire, Surf Master Class and Shaka Surf Women operate in the wider area, and Haven Sports is based in Broad Haven. Beaches and equipment availability change with conditions, so confirm arrangements before travelling. See the Broad Haven surf guide for more.

Broad Haven is a good base for finding waves when the bay is not working. Newgale and Whitesands are around half an hour north, and Freshwater West and Manorbier about 45 minutes south. Our local surf guides cover each: Newgale, Whitesands, Freshwater West and Manorbier.

Explore Broad Haven

Useful additions to a surf-trip plan.

Little Haven

Walk south towards Little Haven by the coast. The beach route depends on the tide, so check tide times and use the coast path when the sand route is cut off.

Newgale and the Coast Path

Newgale is a more exposed beach to assess against the forecast. Broad Haven also sits directly on the Pembrokeshire Coast Path.

Days away from the beach

Options include seasonal Skomer trips, Sweet Home Alpaca, Picton Castle Gardens, St Davids and advance-booked pottery painting in Haverfordwest.

Broad Haven’s northern third has seasonal dog restrictions from 1 May to 30 September. Check current signs when you arrive.
