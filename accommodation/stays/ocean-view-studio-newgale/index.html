Why it works for a Newgale surf trip

The short walk to the beach makes early surf checks and changes in wind or tide easier to respond to. No dedicated board storage or wetsuit-drying facilities are currently confirmed.

Who it is best for

Best suited to one or two guests who value proximity to the beach over extra bedrooms or group space.

Nearby surf

Newgale is the immediate option. Broad Haven and Whitesands provide useful alternatives elsewhere on the Pembrokeshire coast when conditions suggest a change of beach.
