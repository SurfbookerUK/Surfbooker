The Royal Hotel

A historic riverside hotel and comfortable base for exploring Bideford, Westward Ho! and the North Devon coast.

Introduction

A Bideford hotel base for Westward Ho! surf trips.

The Royal sits beside Bideford Bridge and the River Torridge. It is the premium option for staying locally, combining free parking, breakfast and on-site dining with straightforward access to Westward Ho! and the North Devon coast. It is not beachfront.

Food and drink

Eat and drink at The Royal.

Merchants Brasserie serves lunch and dinner, while the Kingsley Lounge offers drinks, coffee and lighter food. Breakfast is available each morning; check current serving times if you are planning an early surf.

Surfing from here

Westward Ho! is the primary nearby surf beach.

Westward Ho!'s long sandy beach and surf are roughly 3.5 miles away by road. The Royal is a driving base rather than beachfront accommodation, and dedicated surf storage or drying facilities are not currently confirmed.
