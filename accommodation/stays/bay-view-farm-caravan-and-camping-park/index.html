At a glance

Practical property details.

Type of stay
Family-run caravan and camping park with pods
Location
Croyde and Putsborough, North Devon
Accommodation
Grass pitches for tents, tourers and campervans (some with electric), camping pods, elite pod, shepherd's hut and a two-bedroom lodge
Suitable for
Families, campers and tourers; limited for dog owners
Confirmed features
Hot showers, family and accessible washrooms, laundry, playground, seasonal shop, takeaway and café, EV charging, Wi-Fi. Wetsuit washing area
Parking
One car per pitch included, extra car charged; units over 5m not accepted
Beach access
About 0.9 km to Croyde beach as the crow flies (Pitchup)
Dogs
Restricted: not allowed in high season (16 July–1 September) or in rental units; confirm with site
