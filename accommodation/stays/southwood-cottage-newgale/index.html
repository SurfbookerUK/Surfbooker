Why it works for a Newgale surf trip

A five-minute walk keeps the beach practical while three bedrooms, a garden and shared living space better suit families or several surfers travelling together. The Pembrokeshire Coast Path runs close to the property.

Who it is best for

A practical choice for up to six guests who need more room than Ocean View Studio and want to remain within walking distance of Newgale Beach.

Nearby surf

Newgale is the closest beach. Broad Haven and Whitesands are useful comparison points when swell, wind or group ability calls for another option.
