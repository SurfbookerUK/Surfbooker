About this destination

Planning a stay in Instow.

Instow works well for visitors who value relaxed waterfront walks, family time and good local food as much as time in the surf. It has an easy estuary-village pace, with Westward Ho! close enough for lessons, hire and surf sessions by car.

Best for

Families, couples, dog-friendly breaks and food-focused stays

Setting

Relaxed estuary village with waterfront views

Getting to the surf

A short drive to Westward Ho! for surf
