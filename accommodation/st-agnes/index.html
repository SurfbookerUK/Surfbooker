About this destination

Planning a stay in St Agnes.

St Agnes suits visitors who value village character and access to the wider coast rather than accommodation directly beside a large resort beach. The village and cove are distinct, so check the exact location before assuming the beach is walkable.

Best for

Village stays, couples and coastal exploring

Setting

Independent village set inland from the cove

Getting to the surf

Trevaunance Cove is the local surf beach; suitability changes with conditions
