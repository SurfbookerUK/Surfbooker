About this destination

Planning a stay in Portrush West Strand.

West Strand — also known as West Bay or Mill Strand — sits right in the middle of Portrush, with the harbour at its northern end and a pedestrian and cycle promenade running along its back. You can walk down from the town centre in minutes, which makes it the easiest beach on the coast to combine with lunch, a wander round the shops or an evening out. It holds Blue Flag status, most recently in 2024.
