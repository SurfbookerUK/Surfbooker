About this destination

Planning a stay in Llangennith.

Llangennith Sands is the northern half of the long sweep of sand that runs up from Rhossili to the tidal island of Burry Holms. Together the bay is about 2.8 miles (4.5km) of beach, backed by dunes and by Rhossili Down, whose summit, the Beacon, is the highest point on the Gower. Surfers simply call this end Llangennith, after the village a short way inland.
