About this destination

Planning a stay in Filey Bay.

Filey Bay is a wide, gently curving bay with around five miles of golden sand, stretching south from the town towards Flamborough. At the town end the beach is backed by a historic promenade with beach chalets and a sculpture trail, and the Coble Landing is where Filey's traditional fishing boats, the cobles, are still kept. The Royal Crescent above the beach dates from the 1850s.
