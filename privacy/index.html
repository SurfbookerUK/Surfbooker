Privacy

Surfbooker uses form submissions to respond to surf lesson enquiries, provider requests and listing updates.

What Surfbooker collects

Surfbooker currently collects the details you submit through the lesson enquiry form, contact form, list-your-surf-school form, accommodation listing form and claim or update form. That typically includes names, email addresses, preferred lesson dates, party details, property details and the message you choose to send.

Surfbooker also includes a lightweight Netlify-backed activity form used for basic event tracking such as listing views, searches and enquiries. No live Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity script is installed on the production files generated in this repository.

How the information is used

Surfbooker uses the data to respond to your request, improve listing quality, understand which pages are being used and route provider or consumer follow-up through hello@surfbooker.com.

If you want a listing corrected, a provider message removed or a request handled manually, email hello@surfbooker.com.
