Northeast England & Yorkshire beach guide

South Shields at a glance

Sandhaven is South Shields' main beach: a long stretch of golden sand backed by dunes, with a promenade running the length of the seafront. It sits south of the Tyne piers and Littlehaven, so you look out across the river mouth towards Tynemouth from the northern end. It holds a Seaside Award, and The Sunday Times named it the UK's beach of the year in 2022.

It's very much a town beach. Behind the sand are Ocean Beach Pleasure Park, the Dunes and South Marine Park with its miniature steam railway, and the seafront has restaurants, cafés and pubs. In summer there are volleyball nets on the sand and regular events, from sandcastle competitions to music.

That makes Sandhaven a good choice when not everyone in your group wants to surf. South Shields Surf School runs surfing and paddleboarding lessons on the beach and hires out equipment, and the Metro gets you here from Newcastle without a car.

Who it suits

  • First lessons with a school on the beach
  • Families with non-surfers
  • Car-free trips by Metro
  • Paddleboarding on flat days

Think twice if…

  • Dog walkers in the daytime between May and September
  • Quiet, wild beach days

Surf guide

Surfing South Shields

Sandhaven is a sandy beach break that faces the North Sea and needs swell from the north-east or east. Westerly winds blow offshore. The Tyne piers give the northern end a bit of shelter, which can make it smaller and cleaner when the open coast is messy.

On small summer days it's a gentle place to learn. South Tyneside Council points out that surfers and kayakers sometimes end up inside the beach safety zone, so if you're on a board, keep clear of swimmers and the flagged area.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Lifeguards

Two RNLI stations

South Tyneside Council says there are two RNLI lifeguard stations on Sandhaven in the high season, usually May to September.

Dogs

8am–6pm ban in summer

Dogs are excluded from Sandhaven between 8am and 6pm from 1 May to 30 September. Outside those hours and dates, they're allowed.

Getting there

Sea Road, NE33 2LD

The beach is on Sea Road. South Shields is the end of the Metro's Yellow line from Newcastle, and the Shields Ferry crosses to North Shields.

Facilities

Seafront

A promenade along the whole seafront, plus restaurants, cafés and pubs. Volleyball nets go up in summer.

Notice

Plastic pellets

After a vessel collision in the Tyne on 19 July 2026, pellets were reported on parts of the coast. Don't handle them, keep dogs on leads in affected areas and follow staff advice.

FAQs

South Shields: quick answers

Is there a surf school in South Shields?

Yes, South Shields Surf School is based on Sandhaven Beach and teaches surfing and paddleboarding, with equipment hire.

Are there lifeguards on Sandhaven?

Yes, two RNLI stations in the high season, usually May to September.

Can dogs go on Sandhaven Beach?

Not between 8am and 6pm from 1 May to 30 September. Outside those times they're allowed.
