Cornwall beach guide

Towan Beach at a glance

Towan is the flat, sandy beach right in the middle of Newquay, at the bottom of a steep hill below the town centre. Newquay Harbour sits on its western side and gives it shelter, which is why Visit Cornwall calls it the town's most sheltered beach. At low tide the sand runs on to Great Western and Tolcarne, and the coves join up into one long stretch you can walk along.

The landmark here is The Island, a rocky outcrop with a house on top, linked to the mainland by a suspension footbridge built in the early 1900s and set high above the beach. Before the house, the island was sold at auction in 1838 and later had a tea room. Today the bridge is privately owned and the house is a holiday rental, so enjoy the view from the sand.

Because the harbour keeps the waves down, Towan is more of a paddling and play beach than a surf spot for most of the year. That makes it a good choice for young children and for days when Fistral is too big. It's a walk-in beach with no parking at the bottom, and the station, shops and cafés are all within easy reach on foot.

Who it suits

  • Families with young children who want calmer water
  • Car-free visitors staying in central Newquay
  • A sheltered option when Fistral is big and stormy
  • Dog owners, as dogs are allowed all year

Think twice if…

  • Surfing in summer while lifeguards are on duty
  • Regular surf: it's flat much of the time
  • Anyone wanting to drive right down to the sand

Surf guide

Surfing Towan Beach

Towan is tucked in behind the harbour and sheltered from most Atlantic swell. The RNLI notes it only really gets surf in stormy conditions, and local surf guides describe it as often flat in summer. When a big winter storm is hammering Fistral, though, smaller, more manageable sets can reach Towan, which is when it comes into its own as a gentle, sheltered option.

Most importantly, the RNLI says surfing isn't allowed on Towan during the summer season while lifeguards are on duty. In those months, use the red and yellow flags for swimming and bodyboarding, and head to Fistral or another beach if you want to surf. Rocks surround parts of the beach, so take care at either end.

Conditions

Storm-day shelter

Sheltered by the harbour from most swell. It can have small, mellow waves when bigger beaches are blown out.

Patrol

Long RNLI season

For 2026 the RNLI listed Easter holiday cover, then daily patrols from 2 May to 27 September, plus some October dates, 10am–6pm. Check this year's dates.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

Town car parks

There's no parking on the sloping road down to the beach. Use a town centre car park; the Cornwall Beach Guide points to a Towan Beach car park reached via the mini-roundabout at the end of Tower Road.

Facilities

Town on the doorstep

Toilets are nearby and cafés and shops are a short walk up the hill.

Lifeguards

RNLI patrol

Swim and bodyboard between the red and yellow flags. No flags means no lifeguards.

Dogs

Allowed all year

The Cornwall Beach Guide lists dogs as allowed all year, and Towan isn't in Cornwall Council's restrictions table. Check signs on the day.

By train

Newquay station

Newquay is the end of the Atlantic Coast Line from Par, and the station is close to the town centre and Towan.

FAQs

Towan Beach: quick answers

Can you surf at Towan Beach?

Not in summer while lifeguards are on duty: the RNLI says surfing isn't allowed then. Outside those times it's sheltered and often flat, though it can have small waves when storms make Fistral too big.

Can you visit The Island at Towan?

The bridge is privately owned and the house on The Island is now a holiday rental, so treat it as private property and enjoy the view from the beach and harbour.

Is Towan good for young children?

Yes. The harbour shelters it, so the water is usually calmer than at Newquay's more exposed beaches. Stay between the red and yellow flags.

Are dogs allowed on Towan Beach?

Yes, local beach guides list dogs as allowed all year, and Towan isn't on Cornwall Council's restrictions list. Check signs when you arrive.
