Scotland beach guide

Thurso Beach at a glance

Thurso Beach is the town's own sandy bay, where the River Thurso meets Thurso Bay and looks out across the Pentland Firth to Hoy in Orkney. A promenade runs along the back, and the harbour sits in the river mouth at one end. The river is 34 miles long and well known for its salmon fishing, and the town grew as an important Norse trading port.

On a clean north-west swell it throws up lefts and rights over sand — fun, fairly consistent and far less consequential than the shallow reef at Thurso East across the river. Thurso has a long Norse history and the UK's most northerly railway station, so it's easy to reach without a car. From the station it's only a short walk down to the sand.

It's rarely the reason surfers travel this far north, but it's a handy option when the reef is too big or busy, and the promenade suits families and non-surfers who would rather watch from dry land. For first lessons, Dunnet Bay is the better choice. Check the forecast and the wind before you set out, and bring plenty of rubber.

Surf guide

Surfing Thurso Beach

Thurso Beach is a fairly exposed beach break with lefts and rights. North-west swell is ideal, and it picks up both groundswell and local windswell. Southerly winds blow offshore, so the best days are a north-west swell with a southerly breeze.

Winter is the most reliable season, with February the standout month. The water is cold year-round — a winter suit, hood, gloves and boots are recommended — and forecasters note that water quality can be suspect, so avoid going in after heavy rain.

Getting there

Train and ferry

Thurso station, on the Far North Line from Inverness, is Britain's most northerly. Scrabster's Orkney ferry terminal is about 1.5 miles west.

Access

Promenade

The promenade suits pushchairs, with a ramp at the western end; reaching the sand isn't fully wheelchair-friendly.

FAQs

Thurso Beach: quick answers

Is Thurso Beach good for beginners?

Only on small, clean days and ideally with instruction. Dunnet Bay is a better place for first lessons.

What conditions work best?

A north-west swell with a southerly offshore wind, most often in winter.

Can I get there by train?

Yes. Thurso station on the Far North Line is a short walk from the beach.

How does it compare to Thurso East?

The beach is a forgiving sandbar break; Thurso East is a powerful, shallow reef for experts only.
