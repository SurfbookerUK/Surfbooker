Scotland beach guide

Dunnet Bay at a glance

Dunnet Bay is a long, north-west-facing beach on the Caithness coast, running about three miles from Dunnet village towards Castletown. High dunes back the sand and Dunnet Forest lies just inland, east of the A836. It's the most beginner-friendly surf spot on this stretch of the north coast, with plenty of room to spread out even when the North Coast 500 is at its busiest.

Above the bay to the north rises Dunnet Head, the most northerly point on mainland Britain, topped by a lighthouse. The area has plenty of history too: Dunnet Church has records going back to 1230, and during the Second World War pilots from RAF Castletown were housed at the hotel by the beach. It's a good place to combine surfing with walks and sightseeing.

The local surf school runs lessons and hire from the beach car park beside the caravan site. There are two wood-fired saunas around the bay, but no public showers or changing rooms, and no lifeguards. Come prepared to change at the car, and treat the more powerful reefs around Thurso as somewhere to watch rather than surf until you're experienced.

Surf guide

Surfing Dunnet Bay

Dunnet picks up north-west swell well, and northerly swells generally work too. South-east winds are offshore. It's mostly sand with a right-hand reef section, and it can work on all tides; the banks change, so ask the school where's best on the day.

Surf is possible all year. Autumn and winter bring bigger swell and short days; summer is better for lessons on smaller days. The length of the bay means you can usually find a quieter, smaller corner if one section looks too lively.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

Beach car park

Next to the caravan and motorhome site, which is also the surf school's meeting point. Check signs for any charges.

Facilities

Toilets only

Public toilets near the car park, but no changing or shower facilities at the beach.

Lifeguards

None

Call 999 or 112 and ask for the Coastguard in an emergency.

Access

Short walk

A few minutes from the car park to the sand, then soft sand beyond.

Winter

Short days

Check daylight as well as the forecast between November and February.

FAQs

Dunnet Bay: quick answers

Is Dunnet Bay good for beginners?

Yes. It's the best beginner beach on the north coast and lessons run from the beach car park. There are no lifeguards.

Are there showers at Dunnet Bay?

No. There are toilets near the car park but no showers or changing rooms.

How long is Dunnet beach?

About three miles, from Dunnet village towards Castletown.

Is Dunnet Head nearby?

Yes. It's just north of the village and is the most northerly point of mainland Britain.
