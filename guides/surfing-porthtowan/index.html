Cornwall beach guide

Porthtowan at a glance

Porthtowan is a small surf village in a valley on Cornwall's north coast, about two miles south-west of St Agnes. The beach is right at the bottom of the village, so the car park, toilets, cafés and beach bars are only a short walk from the sand. Sandstone and slate cliffs frame the bay, and it all sits within the Godrevy Head to St Agnes heritage coast and the Cornish Mining World Heritage Site.

The tide changes everything here. At high water the beach shrinks, a steep bank can build against the promenade and waves break hard close to the shore. As the sea drops back, a wide sweep of firm sand and rock pools opens up, and on a low tide you can walk along the shore to Chapel Porth, below the Wheal Coates engine house. Check tide times before you set off, as the sea can cut off the stretches under the cliffs.

The name is usually translated from Cornish as 'landing place at the sand dunes'. The valley was busy copper country in the 18th and 19th centuries, and Wheal Towan was one of the county's most productive copper mines. By Victorian times Porthtowan had become a bank holiday outing for people from Redruth. Today it holds a Blue Flag, has seasonal RNLI lifeguards and draws a mix of families and surfers.

Who it suits

  • Surf trips where beach, parking and food are all on foot
  • Improvers and intermediates on clean mid-tide days
  • Low-tide walks to Chapel Porth and Wheal Coates
  • Families wanting lifeguards and a play park by the sand

Think twice if…

  • Dog walkers on summer days (10am to 6pm ban)
  • Complete beginners on big or windy days
  • Quiet sessions on summer weekends

Surf guide

Surfing Porthtowan

Porthtowan is an exposed beach break that takes Atlantic swell from the west and north-west, and forecast sites rate it fairly consistent and surfable year-round. Peaks break both left and right off sandbanks that move around, and mid to low tide usually gives the best shape. South-easterly winds are offshore, and the cliffs give a little shelter from south-westerlies.

It gets busy, and it has a serious side. The RNLI has dealt with sudden flash rips here in rough surf, and rip currents are common around gutters in the sand and near the rocks. At higher tides the beach shelves into a fast shorebreak. On bigger days it's one for confident surfers; if you're newer, pick small days, stay between the black and white flags or book a lesson.

Level

Improvers to intermediates

Best for surfers who can handle punchy peaks. Beginners should stick to small days or lessons.

Hazards

Rips, rocks and shorebreak

Rips form around gutters and the rocks, and flash rips can appear in rough surf. Watch the shorebreak at high tide.

Conditions

Mid to low tide, SE wind

Mid to low tide usually works best, with south-easterly winds offshore. Shifting banks mean the best peak moves around.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

Porthtowan Beach Car Park

A large pay-and-display car park just off Beach Road, right beside the sand. There's more paid parking in the village; don't block lanes when it's full.

Facilities

Toilets, showers and a play park

Public toilets on the seafront with seasonal showers, a children's play park at the top of the beach, and beach bars and cafés close by. Ramps give mostly level access, though soft sand and the high-tide bank are harder going.

Lifeguards

Seasonal RNLI patrol

RNLI lifeguards patrol from spring to autumn, daily through the summer. Check the RNLI website for this year's dates, then swim between the red and yellow flags and surf between the black and white.

Dogs

Summer daytime ban

Cornwall Council bans dogs from the beach from 15 May to 30 September, 10am to 6pm. They're allowed outside those times.

By bus

From Truro or Redruth

Visit Cornwall lists the 304 from Truro and the 315 from Redruth. Redruth and Truro have mainline stations; check current timetables before you travel.

FAQs

Porthtowan: quick answers

Can dogs go on Porthtowan beach?

Not between 10am and 6pm from 15 May to 30 September, under Cornwall Council's rules. Outside those hours and dates, dogs are allowed.

Can you walk from Porthtowan to Chapel Porth?

Yes, along the beach at low tide, or at any time on the coast path over the cliffs. If you go by the shore, check tide times first, as the rising tide can cut off the beach below the cliffs.

Is Porthtowan good for beginners?

On small, clean days and inside the lifeguard flags, yes. It's an exposed beach with rips and a heavy shorebreak at high tide, so on bigger or windy days it's better left to experienced surfers or a lesson.

Where do you park at Porthtowan?

Porthtowan Beach Car Park, off Beach Road, is right next to the beach. There's also paid parking in the village.
