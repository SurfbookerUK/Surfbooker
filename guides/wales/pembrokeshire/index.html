Pembrokeshire surf & beach guides

Compare Pembrokeshire surf destinations, including its dependable open beaches, compact coves and more demanding remote options.

Compare the places that genuinely matter to a surf trip.

Beginners to improvers

Newgale

A long, shingle-backed Pembrokeshire beach with broad Atlantic exposure and plenty of room to assess the day.

Experienced surfers

Freshwater West

A powerful, exposed Pembrokeshire beach with a serious reputation and conditions better suited to capable surfers.

Beginners to experienced

Whitesands

A scenic, exposed beach near St Davids with reliable surf appeal and a compact seasonal visitor setup.

Beginners and families

Broad Haven

A village-backed sandy beach that can suit learners and families when the swell and tide are favourable.

Improvers and experienced surfers

Manorbier

A compact Pembrokeshire bay beneath the village and castle, with a punchier surf character than its size suggests.

Conditional beginner sessions

Freshwater East

A sheltered sandy bay that occasionally becomes a useful small-wave or storm-swell alternative.

Experienced, self-sufficient surfers

Marloes Sands

A remote, dramatic beach with Atlantic exposure, a substantial walk and surf that demands independent judgement.

Coached sessions on suitable days

Nolton Haven

A small Pembrokeshire cove with a local activity base and surf potential when swell reaches the bay.
