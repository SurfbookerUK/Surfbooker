Llangennith

A huge, exposed beach at the western end of Gower and one of the best-known surf destinations in Wales.

Capable surfers and scenic trips

Rhossili Bay

The landmark western Gower bay, with a dramatic clifftop approach and the same broad Atlantic sweep as Llangennith.

Beginners and families

Caswell Bay

A compact, accessible Gower bay that can be a practical choice for lessons and smaller-wave sessions.

Langland Bay

A popular Swansea-side reef and beach setup with several wave options when swell reaches the bay.

Occasional sheltered sessions

Oxwich Bay

A long, sheltered south Gower bay that needs the right swell direction to become a genuine surf option.

Occasional small-wave sessions

Port Eynon

A sheltered south Gower village beach that occasionally offers manageable surf when conditions line up.

Newgale

Broad Haven
