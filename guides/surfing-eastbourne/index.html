Southern England beach guide

Eastbourne at a glance

Eastbourne's seafront curves north-east from the foot of the South Downs, with Beachy Head's chalk cliffs rising just west of town. The beach is shingle, with sand showing at low tide, and the Victorian pier sits roughly in the middle. Designed by Eugenius Birch and opened in 1870, the pier lost much of its central building to a fire in 2014, though around two-thirds of the structure was saved.

Beachy Head shelters the bay from the prevailing south-westerlies, which is good news for swimmers and paddlers and bad news for surfers. Surfable waves are rare, so think of Eastbourne as a calm-water base for swimming, kayaking, paddleboarding and sailing rather than a surf beach. The flip side is that on a calm summer morning the water can be glassy and inviting, and the providers along the seafront make it easy to get out on it.

The main resort beach between the Wish Tower and the pier has a seasonal council lifeguard patrol, based just east of the Wish Tower on Grand Parade, which also serves as the first aid post. Parents can pick up free waterproof Kidzsafe wristbands there to write a phone number on. In summer dogs aren't allowed on that stretch, but there are other parts of the seafront where they can go.

Surf guide

Surf and sea

Surf is rare here because Beachy Head blocks most swell. On most days the sea is calm enough for swimming and paddle sports, and providers along the seafront offer kayaking, paddleboarding, sailing and windsurfing in season.

Away from the patrolled beach, Visit Eastbourne points you to its beach safety tips. In an emergency call 999 and ask for the Coastguard.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Lifeguards

Council patrol

In 2025 the patrol covered Wish Tower to the Pier from 24 May to 7 September, with hours varying by month. Station on Grand Parade.

Dogs

Seasonal

1 May–30 September: no dogs Wish Tower to Pier; on leads from Holywell to Harbour Reach; off-lead allowed on Western and Middle Parades if they cause no inconvenience.

Kidzsafe wristbands

Free from the lifeguard station or Seafront Office.

FAQs

Eastbourne: quick answers

Can you surf in Eastbourne?

Rarely. Beachy Head shelters the bay from most swell.

Can dogs go on Eastbourne beach?

Not between the Wish Tower and the Pier from 1 May to 30 September. Elsewhere on the seafront they need to be on leads, except on Western and Middle Parades.

Where are the lifeguards in Eastbourne?

On the main resort beach between the Wish Tower and the Pier, in season, from a station on Grand Parade.
