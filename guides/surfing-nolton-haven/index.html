Wales beach guide

Nolton Haven at a glance

Nolton Haven is a small cove of sand and shingle on the east side of St Brides Bay, tucked between cliffs on the narrow coast road from Broad Haven to Newgale. It faces south-west and is fairly sheltered, with rock pools at the edges and the Pembrokeshire Coast Path running right past it. The Celtic Trail cycle route passes the beach too.

Like Little Haven, it was once a coal-exporting harbour. The flat grassy terrace above the beach was the yard where coal was stored before being loaded onto boats, a small reminder of the Pembrokeshire coalfield that reached the coast here.

Today it is a quiet, easy-going cove with a pub, a slipway and a National Park car park over the road. It is good for a swim, rock pooling or launching a kayak, and the National Park singles it out for photography and sunset reflections. Don't come expecting regular surf, though: the shelter that makes it calm means it rarely has waves when the rest of the bay is small.

Who it suits

  • Families looking for a small beach with easy slipway access
  • Kayakers and paddleboarders on calm days
  • Dog owners, as dogs are allowed all year
  • Photographers chasing a sunset

Think twice if…

  • Surfers looking for regular waves — Newgale is two miles north
  • Anyone needing accessible or baby-changing toilets

Surf guide

Surfing Nolton Haven

Occasional waves only.

Nolton Haven is well tucked in. Most days it is flat or very small, and it only picks up worthwhile waves when a large west or south-west swell is running, often when Newgale is too big. Even then it is a small cove with rocks close by, so it suits confident surfers making the most of a big day rather than beginners.

The National Park warns that currents here can be unpredictable, especially at low tide. In summer, when lifeguards are on duty, stick to the flagged areas. On calm days it is much better treated as a swimming and paddling spot.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

National Park car park

A Pembrokeshire Coast National Park Authority car park across the road from the beach, with seasonal charges. Postcode SA62 3NH.

Facilities

Toilets and a pub

Public toilets in the car park, though there are no disabled or baby-changing facilities. A pub and slipway are by the beach.

Lifeguards

Seasonal

Seasonal lifeguards patrol the beach. Check RNLI dates and swim between the red and yellow flags.

Dogs

Welcome all year

Dogs are allowed year-round. Clean up after them across the whole site.

Getting there

Bus, bike or car

The seasonal Puffin Shuttle coastal bus stops here and the Celtic Trail cycle route passes by. By car, take the coast road between Broad Haven and Newgale.

FAQs

Nolton Haven: quick answers

Can you surf at Nolton Haven?

Only occasionally. The cove is sheltered and needs a big swell. Newgale, two miles north, is the surf beach for this part of St Brides Bay.

Are dogs allowed at Nolton Haven?

Yes, all year. You need to clean up after your dog.

Is Nolton Haven easy to get onto?

Yes. A slipway gives easy access to the beach from beside the car park.

Are there lifeguards?

Yes, seasonally. Check RNLI dates before you visit.
