Choose a beach

Compare suitability, access, facilities and local trip options.

Beginners to experienced

Portrush East Strand

The most practical all-round Portrush choice: a long town beach, established instruction and enough space to match different abilities when conditions cooperate.

Mixed abilities on suitable days

Portrush West Strand

A convenient town-backed beach and useful Portrush alternative, with straightforward facilities and generally more shelter than the open Whiterocks end.

Beginners to experienced

Whiterocks

The standout scenic surf beach near Portrush, combining open Atlantic exposure with excellent facilities and dramatic limestone scenery.

Beginners and improvers

Portstewart Strand

A spacious, family-friendly strand with many peaks and unusually convenient beach parking, but vehicle rules, soft sand and access times need attention.

Beginners and families

Benone Strand

The strongest Northern Ireland choice in this set for a first lesson or mixed family surf day, with space, established instruction and inclusive facilities.

Improvers and experienced surfers

Downhill Beach

A dramatic and more exposed extension of the Benone shoreline, best treated as a condition-led choice rather than the default beginner beach.

Mixed abilities on suitable days

Castlerock

A scenic, quieter village surf base with useful facilities and rail access, but the nearby Bann estuary makes local current awareness important.

Improvers and experienced surfers

White Park Bay

A beautiful coastal walk and wildlife stop, but not a Surfbooker-recommended swimming or beginner-surf destination because the National Trust warns of dangerous rip currents.

Mixed abilities on suitable days

Ballycastle Beach

An attractive, accessible town beach for a flexible family day, but an inconsistent surf destination that requires the right swell direction.
