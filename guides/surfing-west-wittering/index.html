Southern England beach guide

West Wittering at a glance

West Wittering is a long sandy beach at the mouth of Chichester Harbour, privately owned and run by the West Wittering Estate. Behind the dunes is a big grass car park, and at the western end East Head, a National Trust sand and shingle spit, curls into the harbour. On sunny weekends it's one of the busiest beaches on the south coast, and on sold-out days you can only park if you've booked in advance.

Surf at the Witterings is mostly an autumn and winter affair, when Channel swell is more likely and the beach is quiet. In summer the focus shifts to swimming between the lifeguards' red and yellow flags, sailing and windsurfing. Near the harbour mouth, The Hinge is off limits for swimming because of dangerous currents and sandbars, so keep to the patrolled area, and check conditions before you paddle out.

East Head is one of the last natural stretches of coast left in West Sussex and one of the fastest-moving spits in the country. Its dunes, salt marsh and tidal channels are a good walk from the main beach on a mix of soft sand, shingle and boardwalk. Dogs are welcome there on a lead but must be kept out of the dunes during the bird nesting season.

Surf guide

Surfing West Wittering

West Wittering is a sandy beach break that depends on south-westerly Channel swell, which is most likely from autumn to early spring. Summer days are usually small and the water is busy with swimmers, so surfers tend to come out when the crowds have gone.

Close to the harbour entrance, the estate prohibits swimming at The Hinge because of dangerous currents and sandbars. Stay away from that end in the water, and in summer keep surfing and windsurfing clear of the flagged swimming zone.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

Book ahead

Car park open 06:30–20:30. On sold-out days you can't pay at the gate, so book in advance. Disabled bays are by the entrance and opposite the café.

Lifeguards

Estate lifeguards

Mid-May to mid-September, 10am–5pm daily. Swim between the red and yellow flags.

Dogs

Main beach restriction

No dogs on the main swimming beach (groynes 14a–18) from May to 14 September in 2026; allowed elsewhere all year.

Facilities

Toilets, showers, water

Three toilet blocks with accessible toilets (RADAR key from the café or kiosks), two cold showers and drinking water taps.

Access

Ramp and beach wheelchairs

A steep concrete ramp near the windsurf club is the main structured access; the estate has beach wheelchairs.

FAQs

West Wittering: quick answers

Do I need to book parking at West Wittering?

It's recommended, and on sold-out days you can only park with a booking.

Can dogs go on West Wittering beach?

Not on the main swimming beach (groynes 14a–18) from May to 14 September in 2026. They're allowed on the rest of the beach year-round.

Is West Wittering good for surfing?

It can be in autumn and winter, when Channel swell is more likely. Summer is mostly small.

Can you walk to East Head from the beach?

Yes. It's at the western end, reached over sand, shingle and some boardwalk. Dogs must be on leads.
