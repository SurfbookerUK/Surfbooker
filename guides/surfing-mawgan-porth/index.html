Cornwall beach guide

Mawgan Porth at a glance

Mawgan Porth is a small settlement about four miles north of Newquay, where the River Menalhyl reaches the sea at the end of the Vale of Lanherne. The sandy beach is backed by dunes and sheltered by high cliffs at each end. The village sits right behind the sand, with a pub, a general store, hotels and guest houses, so it is an easy park-and-walk beach day.

The river crosses the beach on its way to the sea, so keep an eye on younger children around it, especially as the tide changes. The South West Coast Path runs behind the beach, linking it south to Watergate Bay, just around the headland, and north along the cliffs towards Bedruthan. The coast path makes it easy to combine a surf with a cliff walk, and parking right behind the beach means you can come and go with the tide.

People have lived here for a long time. Excavations between 1949 and 1974 uncovered a late Saxon settlement from roughly 850 to 1050 AD, with courtyard houses and a burial ground. The beach holds a Marine Conservation Society recommendation, and The Beach Guide describes its waves as good and often uncrowded compared with its busier neighbours. Bring a picnic or use the village shop.

Who it suits

  • Families wanting the village on the doorstep
  • Improvers looking for a quieter alternative to Watergate
  • Coast-path walkers

Think twice if…

  • Big-wave surfers on most days
  • Anyone after a long, empty stretch of sand

Surf guide

Surfing Mawgan Porth

Mawgan Porth faces west and works on a west-north-west swell with an easterly, offshore wind. Surf-forecast classes it as a river break, with waves peeling left near the river mouth, and several peaks can form across the bay when the banks are good. On most days it is smaller than Watergate.

Surf-forecast says the tide doesn't greatly affect quality, but it warns of dangerous rips and notes it can get crowded when the surf is working. The river changes the sandbanks, so check the flags and ask the lifeguards before paddling out.

Level

Families and improvers

Rips form on the banks; watch children near the river channel.

Toilets, shops, a pub and a surf school with equipment hire are close to the sand.

Swim between the red and yellow flags when lifeguards are on duty.

Dogs

The Beach Guide lists dogs as allowed all year, and Mawgan Porth isn't in Cornwall Council's restrictions table. Check entrance signs.

FAQs

Mawgan Porth: quick answers

Are dogs allowed at Mawgan Porth?

The Beach Guide lists dogs as allowed all year round, and it isn't in Cornwall Council's restrictions table. Check signs on arrival.

How far is Mawgan Porth from Newquay?

About four miles north, just beyond Watergate Bay.

Is Mawgan Porth quieter than Watergate?

Usually. It is smaller, and The Beach Guide describes its waves as often uncrowded, though it can get busy when the surf is working.
