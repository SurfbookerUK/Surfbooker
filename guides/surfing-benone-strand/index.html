Northern Ireland beach guide

Benone Strand at a glance

Benone Strand is part of a seven-mile run of golden sand on the north coast of County Londonderry, overlooked by the cliffs and mountain of Binevenagh with views across to Donegal. The council describes the sand as clean and firm, with no rocks, shingle or seaweed — a rare thing on an Atlantic coast, and a big reason it works so well for learning.

It sits within the Binevenagh Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, and its dunes are protected as part of the Magilligan Special Area of Conservation. Boardwalks and a waymarked trail cross the dunes from the entrance, and the area is known for its botany, insects and shells. In 2017 it became Northern Ireland's first fully inclusive beach, through the council's work with the Mae Murray Foundation.

Parking is free at the entrance and on the beach itself, and the Ark building at the entrance houses toilets and a coffee dock. The council sets out zones for swimming, jet skis, dogs and other activities, so look at the signs before you set up. With so much sand you can always find space, but facilities thin out quickly as you walk away from the entrance.

Surf guide

Surfing Benone Strand

Benone is an open sandy beach break with lefts and rights spread along the strand. Forecast sites rate the strand best on a north-westerly swell with offshore winds from the south-east. Firm sand and the lack of rocks make it forgiving for learners, but it is still an exposed Atlantic beach and is more demanding as the swell builds.

Banks move after storms, and rips can form between them. Use the zoned areas and stay between the flags when lifeguards are on duty. Further east towards Downhill the beach is a little more exposed.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

Free

At the beach entrance and on the beach, with accessible parking.

Facilities

The Ark

Accessible toilets and a coffee dock at the entrance. Beach access equipment can be booked through the Mae Murray Foundation.

Lifeguards

Seasonal RNLI patrol

The council lists a seasonal RNLI patrol, 10am–6pm daily in July and August. Check the RNLI site for this year's dates.

Dogs

Summer restrictions

Dogs are allowed, with restrictions from 1 June to 15 September (the Blue Flag season). Use the dog zone.

Nature

Protected dunes

Part of the Binevenagh AONB and Magilligan Special Area of Conservation — keep to the boardwalks.

FAQs

Benone Strand: quick answers

Is Benone good for learning to surf?

Yes. The sand is firm and rock-free, there are lifeguards in July and August, and the activity zones keep surfers apart from other users.

Is parking free?

Yes, at the entrance and on the beach.

Is Benone accessible?

It was Northern Ireland's first fully inclusive beach, with accessible parking, toilets and bookable beach equipment.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, with restrictions from 1 June to 15 September.
