Northern Ireland beach guide

Ballycastle Beach at a glance

Ballycastle Beach runs for about 1.2km from the pier at the marina to Pans Rock, mostly sand with some shingle, and backs onto the golf course for most of its length. The view is the big draw: Fair Head to the east, Rathlin Island offshore, and the town behind you. It is about five minutes' walk from the town centre over a footbridge across the River Margy.

There's a promenade at the western end, child-friendly areas, the Children of Lir sculpture on the seafront, and a short boardwalk through the dunes to a small viewing platform. The beach has wheelchair access, accessible parking and toilets, and a visitor information centre nearby. It regularly holds a Seaside Award, most recently in 2024, and RNLI lifeguards patrol in July and August.

The eastern end is part of the Ballycastle Coalfield ASSI, which the council calls the best exposure of a coalfield sequence in Ireland. Coal and iron ore were mined here from the 16th to the 19th centuries, and fossils from fish to giant clubmosses have been found in the rocks. The rocks there date back some 335 million years. For surfers, though, it is an occasional spot that needs the right swell, so treat it as a family beach first.

Surf guide

Surfing Ballycastle Beach

Forecast sites list Ballycastle as a beach break with lefts and rights, best on a north-westerly swell with offshore south-easterly winds. It is more sheltered than the Portrush beaches, so even with a big forecast the bay does not always work.

Watch out for rocks at Pans Rock, the river mouth and harbour traffic at the western end. Use the flagged zones when lifeguards are on duty in July and August.

FAQs

Ballycastle Beach: quick answers

Is Ballycastle good for surfing?

Only on the right swell. Forecast sites favour a north-westerly swell and a south-easterly wind.

Is it lifeguarded?

The council lists a seasonal RNLI patrol, 10am–6pm daily in July and August.

How long is the beach?

About 1.2km, from the marina pier to Pans Rock.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, with restrictions from 1 June to 15 September.
