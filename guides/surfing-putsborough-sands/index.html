Putsborough Sands

Waves, wind protection, beginner suitability, parking, facilities and the drawbacks to know before you visit.

Updated 2 August 2026

A quieter alternative when Woolacombe is blown out.

Putsborough Sands forms the southern end of Woolacombe Beach and sits beneath Baggy Point. It offers the same wide sandy beach but usually feels quieter, less commercial and more sheltered.

Its protection from southerly and south-westerly winds can produce cleaner waves when Woolacombe is blown out, making Putsborough an extremely useful alternative.

What is the surfing like?

Putsborough is a sandy beach break with several left- and right-breaking peaks. The waves are often slightly smaller than at Woolacombe but can be cleaner and better shaped when strong south-westerly winds affect the surrounding beaches.

It can work through the tides, although the quality and position of the peaks change considerably. A rising tide often works well, while the waves can occasionally lose their shape around high tide.

The area beside Baggy Point can produce stronger waves and a useful rip for experienced surfers. Less experienced surfers should stay well away from the rocks at the southern end.

Is Putsborough good for beginners?

Yes. Putsborough can be very good for beginners when the waves are small and manageable. The sandy bottom, quieter atmosphere and protection from some prevailing winds create a good environment for learning.

However, beginners should remain away from the rocks and should not enter the water alone. The beach's own safety guidance recommends staying to the right of the Yellow House and booking a lesson with an approved instructor. Putsborough surfing advice

Importantly, Putsborough does not have regular lifeguard patrols. Visitors should check local safety information, conditions and tide times carefully before entering the water. Putsborough beach information

Parking

Putsborough has a large private car park directly above the beach. Access to the sand is straightforward, making it convenient when carrying surfboards and equipment.

High-season parking currently costs £15 for the day. Lower prices may apply outside peak periods, but the current charge is displayed at the gatehouse when arriving.

There is no cheaper short-stay option, meaning a quick surf can cost the same as an entire beach day. Check current Putsborough parking information

The approach uses narrow, winding country lanes with limited passing places. Drive carefully and expect delays during busy summer periods.

Facilities and atmosphere

Putsborough has a beach cafe, shop, toilets, cold freshwater showers and surfboard and wetsuit hire. Putsborough Beach information

The atmosphere is quieter and more natural than Woolacombe or Croyde. There is no busy village immediately behind the beach, so it feels more like a dedicated surf or beach trip.

The views across Woolacombe Bay are excellent, and the car park's elevated position allows you to check the surf before carrying your equipment down.

The drawbacks

The £15 high-season parking charge is expensive, particularly for a short visit. The narrow access lanes can also be stressful when meeting larger vehicles.

There are fewer food and entertainment options than at Woolacombe, and no village within an easy walk of the beach facilities.

The lack of lifeguard patrols is important for beginners and families. Strong rips can develop near Baggy Point and the rocks at the southern end should be avoided.

Its sheltered position also means Putsborough can be smaller or nearly flat when more exposed beaches are receiving limited swell.

Surfbooker verdict

Putsborough is one of Devon's best alternatives when southerly or south-westerly winds make Woolacombe messy. It offers clean, enjoyable waves, a quieter atmosphere and good conditions for beginners and improving surfers when the sea is manageable.

It does not have Woolacombe's range of facilities or Saunton's extensive space, and the lack of lifeguards means greater personal responsibility is required.

For surfers looking for cleaner conditions, fewer crowds and a more peaceful beach, Putsborough is an excellent choice, provided the forecast shows enough swell.
