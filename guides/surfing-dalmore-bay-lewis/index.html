Dalmore at a glance

Dalmore, Dail Mòr in Gaelic, is a hamlet and sandy bay on the Atlantic coast of Lewis, just north of Carloway. The beach faces north-west, a group of sea stacks stands at its north-east end, and on spring and summer evenings the low sun lights up the bay and cliffs. It lies in the Northside of Carloway.

There is more history here than first appears. Excavations after part of the sea wall collapsed in 1982 found Bronze Age structures and Beaker-period finds, including quartz arrowheads made from stone brought from the mainland. The cemetery behind the beach holds 18 war graves, and both feature in Peter May's Lewis novels. In August 2016 the Transocean Winner oil rig ran aground on the headland just off the beach.

Big Atlantic rollers often run into the bay and it is a long-standing favourite with surfers. It has a car park and public access, and the West Side Coastal Path passes through on its way from Gearrannan to Shawbost. Dalbeg, a smaller beach, is less than a mile along the coast. It's a good base for a day on the west side.

Who it suits

  • Experienced surfers comfortable in Atlantic surf
  • Photographers — the sea stacks and evening light
  • Walkers on the West Side Coastal Path
  • Readers following Peter May's Lewis trail

  • Beginners surfing alone
  • Swimmers on windy or big-swell days

Surf guide

Surfing Dalmore

Dalmore faces north-west into the open Atlantic, so it picks up plenty of swell; large rollers frequently break on the beach. North-west swell is the most-cited ideal, and easterly winds blow offshore. The sand is shaped by the rocks at either end.

There is a strong rip current at the north end of the beach, and Visit Outer Hebrides warns of strong currents and huge waves in windy weather. Autumn to spring brings the most swell; check the forecast and local advice before paddling out.

Surf lessons and hire at Dalmore

Surf Lewis

Surf school & hire, Lewis and Harris
surflewis.com

The main coaching operator on Lewis. Teaches at whichever beach suits the conditions — often not Dalmore. Hire (boards, wetsuits, boots, gloves, hoods, soft racks) must be booked in advance.

There's no surf shop at Dalmore; Stornoway has the island's main shops.

FAQs

Dalmore: quick answers

Is Dalmore safe for swimming?

Only in gentle conditions. There is a strong rip at the north end and strong currents and big waves in windy weather.

Can beginners surf Dalmore?

It is a beach for competent surfers. Beginners should go with a local instructor who can pick the right beach for the day.

What is there to see?

Sea stacks at the north-east end, a cemetery with war graves, Bronze Age archaeology and the West Side Coastal Path.

How far is Dalbeg?

Less than a mile along the coast.
