Wales beach guide

Freshwater East at a glance

Freshwater East is a broad, sandy bay on Pembrokeshire's south coast, a few miles from Pembroke, backed by dunes and sheltered on its southern side by the headland of Trewent Point. The name might suggest a twin to Freshwater West, but the two could hardly be more different: this side faces south-east and is usually calm.

It is a relaxed family beach with a small village a short walk away. There are toilets, a café, a slipway, and a shop and pub nearby. Water quality is rated excellent, it holds a Green Coast Award and it is recommended by the Marine Conservation Society. A small stream crossing the southern end is popular with children. Like several coves along this coast, it was once a favourite of smugglers.

There is no lifeguard service, so take extra care in the water. The coast path runs west towards Stackpole and Barafundle and east to Swanlake Bay and Manorbier, so the beach makes a good starting point for a walk.

Who it suits

  • Families wanting a calm, sandy beach
  • Paddlers, kayakers and swimmers on settled days
  • Walkers heading for Stackpole, Barafundle or Manorbier
  • Surfers looking for shelter when the west coast is blown out

Think twice if…

  • Anyone who wants lifeguards on duty
  • Surfers looking for regular waves

Surf guide

Surfing Freshwater East

An occasional, conditions-dependent spot.

Because Freshwater East faces south-east, it is sheltered from the prevailing westerly swell and wind. It only has surf when a big swell wraps around the coast or when easterly winds whip up local waves.

That shelter can be useful. When strong south-westerlies are blowing out Freshwater West, this side of the peninsula may be cleaner, though usually small. With no lifeguards, only surf here if you are confident and with others.

FAQs

Freshwater East: quick answers

Can you surf at Freshwater East?

Only sometimes. It faces south-east, so it needs a big wrapping swell or easterly winds. Freshwater West is the reliable surf beach.

Are dogs allowed at Freshwater East?

Yes, dogs are allowed on the beach.

Are there lifeguards at Freshwater East?

No, there is no lifeguard service.

What is near Freshwater East?

Swanlake Bay, Stackpole Quay, Manorbier and Barafundle Bay are all within about three miles.
