Scotland beach guide

Machrihanish at a glance

Machrihanish Bay is a long Atlantic-facing beach on the west coast of Kintyre, running about three miles north from Machrihanish village to Westport. From the south end you look out to Gigha, Islay and Jura, and the village is just north of the Mull of Kintyre.

The village has history beyond golf. The links course was laid out by Old Tom Morris, and the painter William McTaggart drew on the beach and views. In 1905 Reginald Fessenden built a radio station here with a 415-foot mast for transatlantic contact; it blew down in a gale in December 1906.

For surfing, Machrihanish is an exposed beach, point and river-mouth break. Islay and Ireland shelter it from a lot of swell, so it needs a clean westerly. When that lines up with an easterly wind, you have miles of sand to choose from, and Westport at the north end is worth checking too.

Who it suits

  • Intermediates on a west swell
  • Beginners in a lesson at Westport
  • Surf-and-golf weekends

Think twice if…

  • Guaranteed waves on any Atlantic swell
  • Lifeguarded swimming

Surf guide

Surfing Machrihanish

Surf-forecast gives a west swell with an easterly offshore wind as the best combination, and mid tide on the rising tide as the best window. Beach peaks give lefts and rights, and the point and river mouth add variety.

It can work at any time of year, but autumn is the season most people target. Because the beach is so exposed, strong winds can quickly spoil it, and the long empty stretches mean you should surf with someone.

Level

Intermediates

Competent beginners on small days; the point is for experienced surfers.

Tide

Mid, rising

The most-cited window on this beach.

Hazards

Rips and isolation

Strong currents, rocky outcrops and few people about.

FAQs

Machrihanish: quick answers

Why is Machrihanish often flat?

Islay and Ireland block much of the Atlantic swell. It needs a west swell; check Westport too.

What's the best tide?

Surf-forecast suggests mid tide on the rising tide.

How long is the beach?

About three miles from Machrihanish village north to Westport.

Is parking free?

Argyll and Bute Council lists both the village and Westport car parks as free.
