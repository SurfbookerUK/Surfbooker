Northeast England & Yorkshire beach guide

Saltburn at a glance

Saltburn-by-the-Sea is a Victorian resort on the Teesside coast. Henry Pease imagined a town on the clifftop in 1858, and his family's company built it. Below the town, the pier opened in 1869 and is the only surviving pleasure pier on the north-east and Yorkshire coast, while a water-balanced cliff tramway still carries you between the promenade and the town.

The beach is a long stretch of sand running from the pier towards Marske, with Huntcliff rising to the east. Skelton Beck flows down through the wooded Valley Gardens and onto the sand near Cat Nab, where the miniature railway starts. It's a good place to spend a whole weekend.

Saltburn has the longest-established surf scene in the north-east, with schools teaching on the lower promenade since 1983. Beginners use the beach breaks either side of the pier. More powerful waves break south of the town and suit experienced surfers, especially in winter and spring.

Surf guide

Surfing Saltburn

The North York Moors National Park describes good beginner beach breaks either side of the pier, best at high tide. Saltburn faces the North Sea, and the usual recipe is north-east to easterly swell with a south-westerly wind blowing offshore.

South of the town, below Huntcliff, the waves get more powerful and are mainly suited to experienced surfers, especially in winter and spring. Keep well clear of the pier legs, and in summer use the flagged areas the RNLI sets up.

Know before you go

Parking

Cat Nab and The Pier

Council car parks: Cat Nab for long stay, The Pier for short stay.

Dogs

Summer exclusion zone

From 1 May to 30 September, dogs shouldn't be exercised between the Hazelgrove access point and the eastern side of the pier. Dogs on leads are welcome on the promenade.

FAQs

Saltburn: quick answers

Can dogs go on Saltburn beach?

Not between the Hazelgrove access point and the eastern side of the pier from 1 May to 30 September. Elsewhere and outside those dates, yes.

Is Saltburn lifeguarded?

Yes, daily from 23 May to 6 September in 2026, 10am–6pm.

Where's the best surf at Saltburn?

Either side of the pier, often best at high tide. Experienced surfers also look south of the town in winter and spring.

Where should I park?

Cat Nab for long stays, or The Pier car park for short stays.
