Wales beach guide

Broad Haven at a glance

Broad Haven (Aberllydan in Welsh) is a small seaside village halfway down St Brides Bay, about 7 miles west of Haverfordwest. The beach runs the length of the village: at high tide it is a fairly narrow strip of sand and pebbles below the esplanade, and at low tide it opens into a wide expanse of firm sand that stretches towards the cliffs at both ends.

Everything is close together — two car parks, toilets, cafés, a pub, a small shop, a surf and watersports shop and a youth hostel all sit on or just behind the seafront. That makes it one of the easiest beaches in Pembrokeshire for a mixed group or a family day where not everyone is surfing.

The surf is less consistent than at exposed Newgale, a few miles north, but that same shelter makes Broad Haven friendlier on bigger days and a sensible place to learn when Newgale is too big. The cliffs here are around 300 million years old and popular with geologists — look for the rock arch known as Den's Door and the Sleek Stone at the north end.

Who it suits

  • Families who want toilets, food and the car close to the sand
  • Beginners and improvers on small to moderate days
  • Groups where some people surf and others walk, swim or rock-pool
  • Anyone wanting a coast-path walk to Little Haven

Think twice if…

  • Quiet, empty sand in peak summer — it is a popular village beach
  • Surfers chasing the most swell — check Newgale first

Surf guide

Surfing Broad Haven

A gentle, sandy beach break that needs a decent west or south-west swell to wake up.

Broad Haven sits further inside St Brides Bay than Newgale, so swell has to wrap in and arrives smaller. On average days that means softer, more manageable waves that suit beginners and longboards; on big days it can be the place that is still surfable when exposed beaches are closed out.

The beach is at its best on the mid to lower tide when the sand is exposed. Light easterly winds are offshore; westerlies blow straight in.

Level

Beginner to intermediate

Usually gentler than Newgale. Learners should still use the lifeguarded area or go with a qualified instructor.

Conditions

Needs a solid west/south-west swell

Sheltered from smaller swells. Best in light easterly winds around mid to low tide.

Hazards

Tides around the headlands

The incoming tide can cut off routes beneath the cliffs and around to Little Haven — check tide times before walking.

Other sports

Paddleboarding, kayaking, windsurfing

Calm days are good for paddlesports; windsurfers use the bay when the wind is up.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

Two car parks, seasonal charges

A large car park at the northern end has toilets and a tarmac path to the beach. A smaller car park at the southern end fills quickly in summer. Charges apply in season — check the signs.

Toilets

At both car parks

Toilets with accessible facilities at both car parks; the southern block also has baby changing. Opening times are set by Pembrokeshire County Council.

Lifeguards

Seasonal patrol

Lifeguards patrol in the summer season. Check the RNLI website for this year's dates and always use the flagged areas.

Dogs

Seasonal restriction at the north end

Dogs are restricted on the northern part of the beach from 1 May to 30 September. The rest of the beach is open to dogs; follow the signs at each access point.

Access

Slipway and ramps

A concrete slipway and a northern ramp lead onto the sand, and an esplanade runs along most of the seafront — one of the more accessible beaches on this coast.

Safety

Don't get cut off

Low tide lets you walk around the headlands towards Little Haven, but the tide comes back fast. Leave plenty of margin.

Beyond the surf

Things to do around Broad Haven

Walk

A short, steep section of the Pembrokeshire Coast Path links Broad Haven with Little Haven — or walk round on the sand at low tide if the tide allows.

FAQs

Broad Haven: quick answers

Is Broad Haven good for beginner surfers?

Often, yes. It is more sheltered than Newgale so waves are usually smaller and softer, and there is a seasonal lifeguard patrol. On bigger days, take a lesson and stay inside the flagged area.

Can dogs go on Broad Haven beach?

Yes, except on the restricted northern section between 1 May and 30 September. Follow the signs at the access points.

Is Broad Haven the same as Broad Haven South?

No. This is the village beach on St Brides Bay. Broad Haven South is a separate National Trust beach near Bosherston on the south coast of Pembrokeshire.

Where should I park?

There are two car parks: a large one at the northern end and a smaller one at the southern end. Both have toilets; seasonal charges apply.

Can I get to Broad Haven by bus?

Yes, the seasonal Puffin Shuttle coastal bus stops at Broad Haven. Check the current timetable before you travel.
