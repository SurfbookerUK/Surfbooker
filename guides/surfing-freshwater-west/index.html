Wales beach guide

Freshwater West at a glance

Freshwater West – 'Fresh West' to locals – is almost a mile of sand on the exposed west coast of the Castlemartin peninsula, south of Milford Haven, reached along the B4319. Behind it lie the big dune systems of Broomhill and Gupton Burrows. At the southern end, the rocky outcrop of Little Furznip, topped by a thatched seaweed-drying hut, separates it from Frainslake Bay.

This is widely seen as Wales's most consistent surf beach, and it has hosted the Welsh National Surfing Championships. It is also a serious place. Strong currents and heavy waves make it unsuitable for bathing, and the tidal range is around 6.5 metres, so the beach changes a great deal through the day. There's no village, just car parks, toilets and Café Môr, which trades from the southern car park from spring to early autumn.

You may recognise the setting from films. The beach appeared in Ridley Scott's Robin Hood and in Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows, where Shell Cottage was built at the foot of the dunes. The dunes, cliffs and the land inland form part of a protected coastline, home to chough, waders and grey seals.

Who it suits

  • Competent surfers looking for the most consistent waves in Wales
  • Learners only with a school that picks the day
  • Walkers and wildlife watchers exploring the dunes and coast path

Think twice if…

  • Swimmers — strong currents make it unsuitable for bathing
  • Unaccompanied beginners

Surf guide

Surfing Freshwater West

Powerful, consistent and exposed.

Freshwater West faces west into the Atlantic and picks up almost every swell that reaches this coast, so there is usually something here when other Pembrokeshire beaches are flat. Shifting sandbanks along the beach give a range of peaks, with rocky sections towards the ends.

That exposure is also its problem: it is often too big or blown out. It is at its best in light easterly winds and on the smaller days of a swell. Strong currents run along the beach, and the waves can break hard close to shore. If it is maxed out, try more sheltered south-coast beaches such as Freshwater East or Manorbier.

Level

Intermediate to advanced

Not a beach for learners on their own. Go with a school that chooses the day and the spot.

Conditions

Any west/south-west swell

The most consistent spot in the area. Light easterly winds are offshore.

Hazards

Strong currents and powerful waves

Currents and swell make it unsuitable for bathing. Stay out unless you are a competent surfer, and use the flags when lifeguards are on duty.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

Car parks above the beach

Parking is limited and gets crowded when the surf is good. Postcode SA71 5AH.

Facilities

Toilets and Café Môr

Toilets on site. Café Môr trades from the southern car park from spring to early autumn.

Lifeguards

Summer service

A lifeguard station has run in summer since 2010. Check RNLI dates.

Dogs

Allowed all year

Dogs are allowed on the beach all year.

Firing range

Castlemartin range

The MoD Castlemartin range lies close by. Firing can be loud, so obey all signs and red flags on the coast path.

Travel

Getting to Freshwater West

By car

B4319

Freshwater West is on the B4319 between Pembroke and Castlemartin. The road crosses the MoD range — follow signage.

By train

Pembroke

The nearest station is Pembroke, about 8 miles away. Public transport to the beach is limited.

FAQs

Freshwater West: quick answers

Is Freshwater West safe for swimming?

No. Strong currents and swell make it unsuitable for bathing. It is a surfing beach for competent surfers.

Where was Shell Cottage from Harry Potter?

It was a set built at the foot of the dunes at Freshwater West for Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows, and later removed.

Are dogs allowed at Freshwater West?

Yes, all year.

Is there a lifeguard at Freshwater West?

Yes, in the summer months. A lifeguard station has run there since 2010.
