Northeast England & Yorkshire beach guide

Whitby at a glance

Whitby sits at the mouth of the River Esk, and its main surf beach is West Cliff Beach, running north-west from the West Pier towards Sandsend. Above it is the West Cliff, the Victorian side of town, with the Pavilion, a statue of Captain Cook and the whalebone arch. Across the harbour, the ruins of Whitby Abbey stand on the East Cliff at the top of the 199 Steps.

The National Park describes West Cliff as a large, shallow-gradient sandy beach with great beginner and intermediate waves, and an ideal place to learn or progress at surfing or paddleboarding. There's a beach management centre with chalet hire, showers, toilets and a café, plus deckchairs and a children's paddling pool. RNLI lifeguards patrol through the summer.

On the other side of the river, Tate Hill is a smaller, sheltered sandy beach where dogs are allowed all year. If you want a longer walk, the sands carry on from West Cliff all the way to Sandsend at low tide. Tate Hill also sits right below the old town, so it's handy if you're staying on the East Cliff side, and both beaches have the abbey as a backdrop.

Surf guide

Surfing Whitby

West Cliff is a sandy beach and pier break. Surf-forecast describes it as fairly exposed with consistent surf at all stages of the tide, best on an east-north-east swell with south-westerly offshore winds. The gentle slope is what makes it so friendly for learning.

The West Pier shapes the town end of the beach, so keep well away from the harbour entrance and its boat traffic. Surf-forecast notes it's often crowded, so give lessons and swimmers room and use the lifeguarded area in season.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Lifeguards

RNLI, 10am–6pm

In 2026 RNLI lifeguards covered weekends and bank holidays from 2 to 17 May, then patrolled daily from 23 May to 6 September.

Dogs

South end banned 1 May–30 Sep

Dogs are banned on West Beach from the West Pier to about halfway along the beach chalets from 1 May to 30 September. Tate Hill beach welcomes dogs all year but they must be on a lead there from 1 May to 30 September, and on the promenade between the Pavilion and the chalets year-round.

Facilities

Beach management centre

Chalet hire, showers, toilets and a café on West Cliff Beach, plus deckchair and windbreak hire.

Walk

Along the sands to Sandsend

The beach runs continuously north-west to Sandsend — check tide times before you set off.

Getting here

Esk Valley line

Whitby's only regular passenger rail link is the Esk Valley line to Middlesbrough; North Yorkshire Moors Railway steam trains run from Pickering in summer.

FAQs

Whitby: quick answers

Is Whitby good for learning to surf?

Yes. The National Park describes West Cliff Beach as a large, gently sloping sandy beach with great beginner and intermediate waves, and there's a surf school on the beach.

Can I take my dog on Whitby beach?

Between 1 May and 30 September dogs are banned from the southern part of West Beach, from the West Pier to about halfway along the chalets. Tate Hill beach on the east side allows dogs all year, on a lead in summer.

When are the lifeguards on?

In 2026 RNLI lifeguards covered weekends from 2 May and patrolled daily from 23 May to 6 September, 10am to 6pm.

What tide is best?

Surf-forecast says West Cliff produces surf at all stages of the tide, though the beach is much narrower at high water.
