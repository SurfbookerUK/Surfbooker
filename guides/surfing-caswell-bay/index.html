Wales beach guide

Caswell Bay at a glance

Caswell Bay is a sandy, half-mile-long bay tucked behind the Mumbles headland on the south Gower coast, about 20 minutes from Swansea city centre. It faces south-west, with cliffs and wooded slopes framing the beach and a coast path running east over the headland to Langland Bay. It is a compact bay, so even on a busy day the sea, the café and the car park are all within easy reach.

It is one of the easiest Gower beaches for a family day. Toilets, outdoor showers, a café, a beach shop and a first aid point sit at the back of the sand, and RNLI lifeguards patrol over the Easter holidays and every day through the summer. A surf school runs lessons here too. That mix of lifeguards, facilities and small, friendly waves is why it is one of the Gower's favourite beaches for a first go on a board.

The one catch is parking. There is a single car park, and on hot days it is often full by mid-morning, so arrive early or come by bus. Dogs are not allowed on the beach from 1 May to 30 September. Outside the summer ban dogs can go on the sand, but they must still be on a lead on the promenade. The beach also gets much smaller at high tide, so check the tide times before you set up for the day.

Surf guide

Surfing Caswell Bay

Caswell needs a decent south-westerly swell to break, but when it does it produces friendly waves that suit learners and improvers. Northerly winds are offshore, and the bay is more sheltered from westerly winds than the exposed west Gower beaches.

The beach shrinks a lot at high tide and the rocks at the edges come more into play, so lower and mid tides usually give more room for both surfing and swimming. In season, stay between the red and yellow flags.

Parking, facilities & safety

Know before you go

Parking

One car park

A single large car park behind the beach. Once it is full there is nowhere else, so arrive before 10am on busy days.

Facilities

Showers, toilets, café

Public toilets, outdoor showers, a first aid point, a café and a beach shop.

Lifeguards

Easter and summer

RNLI lifeguards in 2026 covered the Easter school holidays and daily from 2 May to 13 September, 10am–6pm. No flags means no lifeguards.

Dogs

Banned 1 May–30 Sep

Dogs are not permitted on the beach from 1 May to 30 September inclusive and must be on a lead on the promenade.

FAQs

Caswell Bay: quick answers

Are dogs allowed at Caswell Bay?

Not between 1 May and 30 September. Outside those dates they are welcome, but they must be on a lead on the promenade.

When are there lifeguards at Caswell?

In 2026 the RNLI patrolled over the Easter school holidays and daily from 2 May to 13 September, 10am–6pm. Dates can change, so check before you go.

Is Caswell Bay good for beginners?

Yes. It is relatively sheltered, has lifeguards in season and a surf school runs lessons here.

What time should I arrive to park?

Before 10am on busy days, as there is only one car park.
