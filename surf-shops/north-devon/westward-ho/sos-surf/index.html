Westward Ho! surf shop

SOS Surf

A local surf shop on Golf Links Road in Westward Ho!, close to the beach and village facilities.

Know before you go

Shop information

SOS Surf publishes its address as Golf Links Road, Westward Ho!, Devon, EX39 1LH. Its currently published hours are Monday to Saturday 9am-5pm and Sunday 10am-4pm.

Opening arrangements can change, so check on the day before making a special journey. Surfbooker does not currently list SOS Surf as a lesson or equipment-hire provider.
