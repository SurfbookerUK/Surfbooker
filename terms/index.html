Terms

Surfbooker is a comparison and enquiry site, not the lesson operator.

What Surfbooker does

Surfbooker compares public lesson information, creates internal school and location pages, and forwards enquiries to the Surfbooker inbox. Surfbooker does not currently process live lesson availability, confirm bookings or take direct payment on the production files in this repository.

What the surf school confirms

Each school remains responsible for lesson delivery, availability, final pricing, waivers, refunds, cancellations, safety procedures and booking confirmation. You should confirm final lesson details directly with the provider before attending.

Surfbooker lists surf schools independently, compares details found on provider websites and passes availability requests to the Surfbooker inbox. Prices, lesson times, booking terms and final availability are confirmed by each school. Surfbooker does not take payment at this stage.
