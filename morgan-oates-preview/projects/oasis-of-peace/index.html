Oasis of Peace

Created for a couple who live in step with nature and wildlife, this garden draws on Moroccan and Mediterranean ideas: patterned tiling, moving water and fragrant planting chosen for pollinators.

A shallow pool encircles a central island, where visitors can slip off their shoes and cool their feet on a hot day. Behind the bench, a sculpture of three oversized elements is lit by day and night.
