About

A small studio that designs, builds and looks after gardens

Morgan Oates is a landscape gardening and garden design company based in Chiswick, West London, working across West, Central and South London and further afield on request.

Director

Daniel Morgan Oates

Daniel began landscape gardening while still at school and grew up in Herefordshire. He studied at Pershore College, worked in West London, and went on to found Morgan Oates.

He leads hard and soft landscaping, and is the person most clients deal with on site.

Garden Designer

Anna Pawlowska

Anna holds a master’s degree in landscape architecture from the University of Warmia and Mazury in Poland, and has studied on scholarships in Hungary, Portugal and Ukraine.

She joined Morgan Oates in 2013, moving from maintenance team leader to maintenance manager, then garden designer. She now leads garden design and soft landscaping projects. Her Moroccan-inspired design was one of five APL Avenue Show Gardens at BBC Gardeners’ World Live in 2019.

The crew

The people on site are friendly, experienced and professional. Site foremen oversee health and safety, keep sites tidy and keep noise down. Project management is about regular communication: if something comes up, you hear about it from us.

Design, build and aftercare

We take on garden design, the construction of designs by others, and maintenance of established gardens, together or separately. See the services, or get in touch.
