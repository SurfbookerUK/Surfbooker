Design

Garden design

A design that comes from the site and the people who will use it, drawn up in stages and carried through to the finished garden.

How the design service works

Good design develops in stages, and we think the designer should see the space and meet you face to face. Depending on what you need, the service can include drawing, planning, surveying, planting design and site management, and it can run on into construction and aftercare.

The stages

  1. 01

    Consultation

    An on-site meeting to understand the space and what you want from it. We write this up as the brief.

  2. 02

    Survey

    Once the brief is accepted, a site survey is arranged, unless you already have one.

  3. 03

    Concept

    Mood boards and 3D visuals show the proposed style, planting and landscape ideas. Plants and materials are not yet fixed.

  4. 04

    Master plan

    When the concept is approved, a scaled, detailed plan and written specification are produced for construction.

  5. 05

    Project monitoring

    The designer oversees the build to check it follows the plan. Any additional services are quoted in advance and charged separately.

Who designs

Anna Pawlowska leads garden design and soft landscaping at Morgan Oates. Her Moroccan-inspired design Oasis of Peace was one of five APL Avenue Show Gardens at BBC Gardeners’ World Live in 2019.
