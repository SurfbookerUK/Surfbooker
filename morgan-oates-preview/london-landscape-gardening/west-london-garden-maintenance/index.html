Care

Garden maintenance

Regular visits that keep an established garden in good condition all year, on a schedule that suits you.

How visits work

Our maintenance team visits regularly and agrees a schedule with you that fits your calendar. Tasks change with the season, and the team assesses what is needed at the start of each visit. Visits are weekly, fortnightly or monthly, depending on the size of the garden.

Example maintenance schedule

Weekly

  • Pressure washing of patios and driveways
  • Hedge cutting and trimming
  • Leaf clearing
  • Shrub and rose pruning

Fortnightly

  • Lawn mowing
  • Pressure washing of patios and driveways
  • Weeding
  • Hedge cutting and trimming
  • Leaf clearing
  • Shrub and rose pruning
  • Lawn care, fertilising and feeding

Monthly

  • Lawn mowing
  • Pressure washing of patios and driveways
  • Weeding and planting
  • Hedge cutting and trimming
  • Leaf clearing
  • Shrub and rose pruning
  • Lawn care, fertilising and feeding
  • Tree maintenance

An example only. Tasks vary with the season and the garden, and are agreed with you.

Maintenance consultancy

Many clients use the service because their lives leave no time for the garden. Others want to do some or all of it themselves, and we can help and advise. We can also carry out a six-month garden health check.
