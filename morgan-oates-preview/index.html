The practice

Design, construction and care under one roof

A garden is judged on the first day and again in the fifth year. The levels are right or they are not; the paving drains or it does not; the planting was chosen for that soil and that light, or it was not.

Keeping design, building and maintenance in one practice means the person who draws a detail is near the person who has to build it, and the people who maintain the garden have often seen it made. Where another designer’s plans are the starting point, we build them with the same care, and we say so plainly on each project.

The people you will deal with
