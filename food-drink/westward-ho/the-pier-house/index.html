Our review

The Pier House is where we go in Westward Ho! when the meal is for a special occasion. Its position on the cliff gives it a real sense of arrival, and the outlook across Bideford Bay makes the setting feel different from an everyday pub meal.

The view is the star. On the right evening, you can watch the light change across the water and the sun drop towards the horizon. The restaurant interior is comfortable and polished without feeling overly formal, so it works for anything from a family celebration to a meal for two.

It is also one of the more practical local options for a large group. The Pier House advertises dedicated spaces for birthdays, parties, gala dinners and other celebrations, while the main restaurant, bar and terraces give groups several ways to use the venue. Contact the team ahead of time so they can match your party to the right space.

The official menu is built around modern English cooking and familiar favourites, with seafood, vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices as well as a children’s menu. There is also a Sunday carvery, plus coffee and homemade cakes earlier in the day.

Our Westward Ho! choice for special occasions, large groups and a great sea view.

Why it works for an occasion

The combination of a proper restaurant, a relaxed bar, outdoor sunset terraces and the sea view means the venue can feel celebratory without everyone needing to dress formally. It works well when a group includes different ages or people who want different levels of formality.

For a birthday, family gathering or larger celebration, arrange it directly rather than relying on a walk-in table. The Pier House promotes a dedicated Bay Suite for events, and its team can advise on room choice, menus and group requirements.

Go here if

  • You are planning a special occasion or family celebration.
  • You need somewhere that can accommodate a large group.
  • You want a meal or drinks with a strong sea and sunset view.
  • Your group needs a varied menu, including children’s and dietary options.
  • You would value onsite parking and a choice of restaurant, bar or terrace seating.

Worth knowing

  • Book restaurant tables and large groups in advance.
  • Window tables cannot be guaranteed or allocated in advance.
  • Terrace dining depends on the weather.
  • Dogs are limited to the Sunset Bar and Sun Terrace, except guide dogs.
  • Check current food-service times before making a special trip.

Getting there and booking

The Pier House is on Merley Road, Westward Ho!, Bideford EX39 1JU. It sits above the beach on the western side of the village and has complimentary onsite customer parking.

Restaurant booking is recommended. Call 01237 477777, email thepierhouse@braddicks.com or use The Pier House website for the latest menu, times and reservations.

Quick answers

Questions about The Pier House.

Is The Pier House good for a special occasion?

Yes. We use it for special occasions because the clifftop setting, restaurant and sunset view give it a stronger sense of occasion than a casual beach stop.

Can The Pier House accommodate large groups?

Yes. The venue hosts parties, celebrations and larger events. Contact The Pier House directly so the team can arrange the right space and menu for your group.

Do you need to book The Pier House?

Booking is recommended for the restaurant. The bar and terraces normally operate without table bookings, but arrangements can change.

Can you reserve a window table?

The Pier House says window tables cannot be pre-allocated. The sea view remains a major part of the wider venue and terrace setting.

Is The Pier House dog friendly?

Dogs are welcome in the Sunset Bar and on the Sun Terrace. They are not permitted in the Bistro, except for guide dogs.

Does The Pier House have parking?

Yes. Complimentary onsite customer parking is available.
