Our review

Tea on the Green is not somewhere you visit for a restrained slice of cake. The cabinet is packed with tall, inventive bakes and the slices are famously substantial. That sense of fun runs through the whole tea room, from the bold cakes to the walls filled with framed pictures, film memorabilia and collections of teapots.

The cakes are the main reason we come. There is usually plenty to deliberate over, and our photos show just how far the selection goes: Biscoff, Creme Egg and Toblerone-inspired creations alongside layered sponges, cheesecakes and other changing specials. A slice with a tea or coffee is enough to make this a destination rather than simply somewhere convenient near the beach.

It is also a proper Westward Ho! institution. Tea on the Green opened in 2008 and has built a reputation well beyond the village for home-baked treats, big portions and high tea. The location gives you an easy stroll to the promenade afterwards, which may be exactly what you need.

Come for the cake cabinet; stay for the wonderfully eccentric surroundings.

Go here if

  • You want cake to be the main event.
  • You enjoy quirky, memorabilia-filled interiors.
  • You are planning high tea or a treat with friends or family.
  • You want somewhere close to the Westward Ho! promenade.

Good to know

  • The portions can be very large, so sharing may make sense.
  • It is popular and can be busy; booking ahead is sensible.
  • Cake flavours and availability change from day to day.
  • Check current opening times directly before making a special journey.

Getting there

Tea on the Green is at Golf Links Road, Westward Ho!, Devon EX39 1LH. It is in the village centre, close to the promenade and an easy addition to a beach day.

For bookings and the latest information, call 01237 429406 or visit the official Tea on the Green website.

Before you visit

Opening days and hours can change seasonally. Check directly with Tea on the Green before travelling, particularly outside the main holiday periods.

High tea and busy weekend visits are best planned in advance.

Quick answers

Questions about Tea on the Green

What is Tea on the Green known for?

It is especially well known for its home-baked cakes, substantial portions, high tea and distinctive memorabilia-filled interior.

Where is Tea on the Green?

It is on Golf Links Road, Westward Ho!, Devon EX39 1LH, close to the village centre and seafront.

Should I book?

The tea room can be very popular, so booking ahead is sensible, particularly for high tea or a busy weekend visit.
