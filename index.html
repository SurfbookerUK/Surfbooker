Is surf equipment included?

Most listed schools say a surfboard and wetsuit are included. Some also publish extra equipment such as boots, gloves, hoods, changing facilities or storage, which you can compare on each listing and school page.

Which North Devon beach is best for beginners?

Surfbooker lists beginner lesson options across Westward Ho!, Saunton, Woolacombe, Croyde and Putsborough. The right beach depends on the school, lesson format and the day's conditions, so compare the school pages and then enquire.

Can children take surf lessons?

Yes. Several listed schools publish children, family or younger-surfer options, but the published age guidance varies by provider. Use the adults and children filter together with the minimum age filter, then confirm the fit with the school.

Should I choose a group or private lesson?

Group lessons usually show the lowest public starting prices, while private lessons can suit families, younger children or surfers who want more focused coaching. Compare the format, duration and facilities on each listing before you enquire.

How is lesson availability confirmed?

Your preferred lesson date is sent with your Surfbooker availability request. The provider then confirms final lesson times, spaces and pricing.

Does Surfbooker take payment?

No. Surfbooker does not take payment at this stage. The school confirms next steps and any payment directly.

Surfbooker lists surf schools independently, compares details found on provider websites and passes availability requests to the Surfbooker inbox. Prices, lesson times, booking terms and final availability are confirmed by each school. Surfbooker does not take payment at this stage.
