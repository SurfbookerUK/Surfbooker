Frequently asked questions

Practical questions about the current Surfbooker flow.

Does Surfbooker check live lesson availability?

No. The preferred lesson date is an enquiry field only. The provider confirms final availability and lesson times.

What does the price filter use?

Surfbooker uses verified public starting prices where they are clearly shown on an official school page. If a school does not publish a clear public starting price, it stays outside price filtering.

Can I compare lesson duration?

Yes. Use the duration sort to bring shorter or longer published lesson formats to the top of the current result set.

How do providers correct a listing?

Providers can use the mailto link on each listing or the footer claim page to send an update to hello@surfbooker.com.
