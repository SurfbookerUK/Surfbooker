Northern Ireland Surf Lessons

Places

Northern Ireland surf lesson places.

Portrush East Strand

Portrush's long town beach — 2.5km of sand running east from the town to Whiterocks, with surf schools, a big car park and the rest of the Causeway Coast on the doorstep.

Portrush West Strand

Portrush's west-facing town beach, between the harbour and the promenade — the easiest place on the coast to combine a surf with food and a night out.

Whiterocks

A long sandy beach beneath sculpted white limestone cliffs, caves and arches — the most scenic surf beach near Portrush, with free parking and good facilities.

Portstewart Strand

Two miles of National Trust sand backed by ancient dunes, where you can park on the beach itself — spacious, family-friendly and full of shifting peaks.

Benone Strand

Seven miles of firm, mostly rock-free sand beneath Binevenagh — Northern Ireland's best beach for a first lesson or a mixed family surf day.

Downhill Beach

The dramatic eastern end of the seven-mile strand, directly beneath Mussenden Temple — more exposed than Benone and better for confident surfers.

Castlerock

A quiet village beach between the Downhill cliffs and the River Bann, with a railway station, a promenade and dunes behind.

White Park Bay

A secluded arc of sand and ancient dunes between two North Antrim headlands — a beautiful walk, but not a place to swim or learn to surf.

Ballycastle Beach

A sand-and-shingle town beach facing Rathlin Island and Fair Head — a great family base, but an occasional surf spot that needs the right swell.
