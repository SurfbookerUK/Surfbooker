Scotland Surf Lessons

Places

Scotland surf lesson places.

Thurso East

A powerful right-hand reef break over flat slab stone at the mouth of the River Thurso — one of Europe's great cold-water waves, and strictly for experienced reef surfers.

Thurso Beach

Thurso's sandy town beach, right below the promenade — a much gentler option than the famous reef round the corner, with the town's cafés and hotels behind you.

Dunnet Bay

A two-mile sweep of sand on Scotland's north coast near Thurso — the best place on this coast to learn, with a surf school on the beach and the cliffs of Dunnet Head at the end.

Melvich

A quiet north-coast bay where the Halladale River meets the sea, with long waves over a river-fed sandbar for intermediates — and very little else around.

Pease Bay

A compact, steep-sided bay with red-rock headlands and a holiday park behind the sand — and some of the most defined beach-break peaks on Scotland's east coast.

Belhaven Bay

A broad sandy bay inside John Muir Country Park, a minute from Dunbar — with a purpose-built surf centre, parking and showers, and one of Scotland's easiest places to learn.

Coldingham Bay

A small sandy cove with beach huts, rocky headlands and a surf school on the sand — and, unusually for Scotland, a seasonal RNLI lifeguard patrol.

Fraserburgh

A long sandy beach right beside a working fishing town, with peaks along the sand for learners and a harbour-end point for better surfers.

Lossiemouth

A friendly Moray surf town with a broad sandy East Beach reached by a footbridge, a surf school on the sand and cafés a few minutes away.

Machrihanish

Miles of Atlantic-facing sand on the Kintyre peninsula, with a village and golf links at the south end and Westport's surf school at the north.

Balevullin, Tiree

A west-facing Atlantic beach on Tiree with a surf school's beach hut right on the sand — and, because the island has beaches facing every direction, somewhere else to go when it doesn't work.

Dalmore Bay, Lewis

A dramatic west-coast Lewis beach with dunes, sea stacks and the full force of the Atlantic — a beautiful place to visit and a serious place to surf.
