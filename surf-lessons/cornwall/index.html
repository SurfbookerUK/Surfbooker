Mawgan Porth

A compact sandy bay in its own valley between Newquay and Padstow, with the village, food and parking right by the beach.

Constantine Bay

A wide, dune-backed bay three miles west of Padstow, fully open to the Atlantic — beautiful, powerful and not a default beginner beach.

Crantock Beach

A long National Trust beach across the Gannel estuary from Newquay — dunes, a sandy bay and a river channel that demands respect.

Fistral Beach

Britain's best-known surf beach — Atlantic-facing, competition-tested and lined with surf schools, hire and food.

Great Western Beach

A sandy town beach below Newquay's station side, with established surf schools and a steep walk back up.

Porthmeor Beach

St Ives' Atlantic-side beach — a compact surf break below Tate St Ives, with a surf school on the sand and the town on foot.

Summerleaze Beach

Bude's town beach — a wide sandy bay at the mouth of the river and canal, with surf schools, the famous sea pool and the town a short walk away.

Towan Beach

Newquay's central beach beside the harbour and The Island's suspension bridge — the easiest surf beach to reach on foot from town.

Trevaunance Cove

St Agnes' surf cove — a compact beach below a steep village valley, with a surf school, pubs and mining-heritage cliffs.
