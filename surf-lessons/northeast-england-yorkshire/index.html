Places

Northeast England & Yorkshire surf lesson places.

Tynemouth Longsands

The north-east's best-known surf beach — half a mile of sand below Tynemouth's seafront, with two surf schools, lifeguards in summer and a Metro station up the road.

South Shields

South Shields' big town beach at the mouth of the Tyne, with a community surf school, summer lifeguards and a seafront full of family attractions.

Bamburgh

Wide white sand below Bamburgh Castle — one of Northumberland's most consistent and most beautiful breaks, with a gentle sandy slope and a left off the rocks at the north end.

Beadnell Bay

A long, curving sandy bay south of Beadnell's tiny harbour — one of Northumberland's best-equipped watersports beaches when the North Sea delivers.

Druridge Bay

Seven miles of empty sand and dunes between Amble and Cresswell — space and solitude, with a country park, café and toilets at its centre.

Saltburn

The north-east's classic surf town — beach breaks either side of a Victorian pier, a surf school on the lower promenade since 1983, and a cliff tramway up to town.

Redcar

A long Teesside seafront with several reef- and sand-influenced peaks — a convenient local option when the swell lines up, with Saltburn's surf schools ten minutes away.

Scarborough North Bay

Scarborough's quieter bay, below the castle headland — gentle enough for learners, with surf schools in town and a classic seaside resort around it.

Cayton Bay

A wide wooded bay just south of Scarborough with several breaks, a year-round surf school on the clifftop and a reputation for the most powerful waves on this stretch.

Filey Bay

Five miles of sand curving south from Filey Brigg — a family resort beach that can produce approachable surf when the North Sea cooperates.

Whitby

A big, gently shelving sandy beach below Whitby's West Cliff, with a surf school on the sand, beach huts and the abbey across the harbour.

Sandsend

A pretty village beach where two streams meet the sea, at the north end of the long sands from Whitby — with beginner-friendly breaks and summer lifeguards.

Runswick Bay

A curved, sheltered sandy bay below a tumble of red-roofed cottages — more of a picture-postcard beach than a surf spot, with occasional waves when swell wraps in.
